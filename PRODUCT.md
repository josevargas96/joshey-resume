# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Jose Vargas for Product Manager roles. They land on the site while screening candidates and deciding whether to reach out or advance him in a process.

## Product Purpose

A personal resume/portfolio site (joshey.io) that stands in for a static resume or LinkedIn profile during candidate screening. Success is a recruiter/hiring manager coming away convinced of concrete product substance and reaching out or advancing him.

## Positioning

Proves concrete product outcomes rather than a list of job titles: payments, telehealth, and AI-powered workflow work with real revenue/business impact. What a generic resume or LinkedIn profile can't show.

## Operating Context

Hosted on GitHub Pages at joshey.io via a CNAME. Currently leading telehealth product for North America at hear.com; hero status line reads "Open to PM roles." Primary CTAs: email contact, jump to work, jump to an about/chat section.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS only, no framework — must stay deployable via GitHub Pages with zero build step.
- A separate Cloudflare Worker (`worker/`) powers a resume-chat feature (OpenRouter-backed).
- Current employer/role facts (hear.com, telehealth, NA) must stay accurate, not embellished.

## Evidence on Hand

Real work history and outcomes only (payments, telehealth, AI workflow projects) as already documented in index.html. No case studies, press, or third-party testimonials on hand — future work must not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Prove substance with concrete outcomes, not adjectives or titles.
- Never fabricate testimonials, metrics, case studies, or embellish current role facts.
- Keep the stack static and framework-free so deployment stays simple.
- Recruiter/hiring-manager screening is the primary success path; design and copy should serve that read quickly.
