01 hear.com Product Manager Jan 2024 — Present

Matching customers to the right provider, automatically

Problem

Appointment scheduling assigned customers to whoever was free. Providers with the wrong capability profile got booked anyway, capacity sat idle, and conversion suffered — a cost problem and a revenue problem wearing the same hat.

Decision

I defined the solution architecture for a Provider Recommendation Service that matched customers to providers on availability and capability, then drove full-cycle delivery with my engineering team. Once it shipped, I extended it with a performance scoring layer that prioritized providers with higher closing rates and lower return rates.

Outcome

Routing stopped being a coin flip. Wasted capacity on salaried providers dropped, and the revenue impact was the largest single contribution of my career so far.

+$5.7M annual revenue −$150K annual cost
  • Owned the US market adaptation of a ground-up telehealth platform rebuild, mapping the new platform to North American provider workflows — contributing to a ~40% reduction in average appointment duration (75 → 45 min) and ~83 provider hours freed weekly across the network.
  • Identified manual clinical note-writing as a top provider time drain and defined the product strategy for an LLM-powered transcription pipeline that auto-generates outcome notes — saving ~5 min across ~180 daily appointments (~3,900 provider hours/year) and establishing the company's first data layer for provider coaching.
  • Owned API integrations and asynchronous messaging architecture across all five applications, including Salesforce CRM integration.
  • Built and maintained Snowflake-connected datasets and SQL queries in Domo to power KPI dashboards; used Mixpanel, LogRocket, and New Relic for behavior analysis and system monitoring.
02 hear.com Associate Product Manager Jan 2022 — Dec 2023

One payment platform instead of four manual processes

Problem

FinOps was running payments through a patchwork of manual steps across disconnected providers. Every transaction carried human overhead, and disputes were handled ad hoc with no defined resolution path.

Decision

I led development of a unified payment platform integrating the Stripe and Allegro APIs, and wrote the product requirements for chargeback and dispute management flows so exposure was handled by process rather than by whoever picked up the ticket.

Outcome

Adoption hit 95% — the number I care most about here, because a payments tool nobody uses is just a second patchwork.

03 hear.com ERP Support Specialist Nov 2020 — Jan 2022

Where I learned to translate between users and engineers

Problem

US ERP users and the development team in Berlin were separated by time zones, language, and vocabulary. Issues took days to resolve because nobody owned the translation between what users described and what engineers could act on.

Decision

I built independent troubleshooting depth and created a structured escalation process between the two sides. As I got more comfortable technically, I started helping our PM gather requirements from business units directly.

Outcome

Average resolution dropped from several days to one or two. More importantly, this is where I found the work I actually wanted to do.
